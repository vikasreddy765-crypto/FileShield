BUILT FOR TRUST

Simple for lecturers. Serious about integrity.

FileShield is designed for colleges and educators who need a clear way to detect changes in digital attendance records. It checks file integrity rather than analyzing student performance.

🔒 Privacy by design
Verification uploads are deleted after hashing. Production deployments should add institutional privacy controls, encrypted storage and an approved retention policy.
